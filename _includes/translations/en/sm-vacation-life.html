"I have to go back, I have a dinner engagement tonight."

"...Mm."

"Xin Zhi."

"Hmm?"

Saki stood by the table, looking down at me. Sunlight streamed through the café's glass windows, falling on her face. In that moment, she was once again the girl from two years ago, wearing short shorts and high heels, walking down the street and turning heads.

"Kneel properly." She said, "You look much better kneeling than when you're pretending to be an S."

She left.

I sat in the café, watching her back disappear around the corner.

The phone vibrated. It was a message from Shen Xue:

"This Saturday, 1902. Queen Mayuko said that Queen Mio will also be coming."

Shook again:

"No being late. M-man."

I replied with a single word:

"Yes."

Then, I picked up the coffee cup and finished the last sip.

Outside the window, the spring sunlight is very good.
