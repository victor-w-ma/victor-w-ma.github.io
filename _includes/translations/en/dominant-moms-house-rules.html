Liu Yue, naked from the waist down, handed her the socks. Xiao Wei took them under the light to smell, then threw them back.

"Tonight is lucky. If there’s news from a boy tomorrow, the echo device will have its mouth covered with these socks, only allowed to repeat what I say."

Xiaowei leaned against the head of the bed, her eyes cold. Her foot tapped on Hemiao's back.

"From today onwards, do this every day. Whoever dares to tell outsiders——Liu Yue, repeat it."

Liu Yue felt her throat tighten: 'From today on, every day like this. Whoever dares to tell outsiders—'

"Before moving out, I’ll first teach her how to behave as a person."

"Before moving out, I’ll first teach her how to behave as a person."

Before the light was turned off, she still used He Miao as a stool. If the pressure was too light, she would grind her shoulder with her heel.

"Heavier. The little bitch is lying all loose and sloppy. The short stool should be knelt on here. If it sags any further, you'll be kneeling to sleep tonight."

He Miao's back immediately tensed. Even if it was correct, she didn't praise. Xiao Wei kept her eyes closed, her lips very thin.

Here, she is the one giving orders. She presses hatred into the palm of her hand, strikes others with it, and steps on others' backs. After hitting, the crack remains. Only tonight, no one can hear it.

7. Position

After moving in, she quickly felt it was not enough. Beating, scolding, inspecting—everything was the actions taught by others. The dormitory was not that home. There were no rulers, no formal greetings, and her three roommates didn’t even know where those words came from. What she wanted were rules she made herself.

On a weekend afternoon, the hallway was quiet. She locked the door, and dragged the chair to the middle of the room.

"From today onwards, you three will have positions. It's not as simple as taking turns mopping the floor. Liu Yue, before you repeat my next sentence, no one is allowed to move."

Liu Yue stood by the door, her spine already beginning to collapse. She straightened herself up again.

Xiaowei looked at Hemiao.

"You, furniture. If a stool is needed, kneel to become a stool; if a table is needed, straighten your back. Little bitch, crawl when going out; if you open the door with your hand, start over. Go, lie down in front of the chair."

He Miao crawled over, on his knees and elbows. Xiao Wei placed two thick professional books and a pen on her back, opened her notebook, and actually wrote two lines of words. The handwriting swayed slightly with her breathing.

"What are you shaking for? The table won't breathe for the owner to see."

He Miao held back her anger. The book on her back still slipped an inch. Xiaowei pulled the pen cap out and tapped her shoulder with it—light, but accurate.

"If it slips again, hold the book with your teeth. A low stool that can't even stay flat is only good for holding with your teeth."

Then she looked at Liu Yue.

"You, throat. I only speak to you. Pass on my words to them exactly as I say. If you misquote a single word, the punishment falls on you. Weakling, first clamp these two dictionaries at the small of your back and stand straight. If they fall off once, start over and repeat all the rules for today from the beginning."

Liu Yue stuffed the dictionary into her waistband, the edges of the paper pressing against her flesh. She didn't dare to slouch. Xiao Wei said casually:

"He Miao, turn half a circle, face the window."

Liu Yue immediately said to He Miao's back: 'He Miao, turn half a circle, face the window.'

He Miao crawled around. The book slipped again. Xiao Wei didn’t look at her, only asked Liu Yue:

"Did I ask you to explain?"

"No."

"For the extra characters in the echo, lick the floor clean with your tongue tonight. Now, report the time. Report it every five minutes. If you report it wrong, whether the dictionary falls or not is on you."

Liu Yue stared at her phone: 'Two seventeen.' Her voice was tight, as if truly hollowed out, leaving only an echo.

Finally, it was Chen Rou. She stood by the sink, twisting the corner of her clothes with her fingers. Even before she was called on, her eyes were already red.

"Your hand is soft. Tonight we won't practice the mouth, we'll practice the hand." Xiaowei took the hanger apart, placing the metal hook into Chen Rou's palm. "Spank He Miao. Spank the part of her back where I wrote. Lighter doesn't count. If you can't bring yourself to do it, just let Liu Yue keep yelling until you do."

Chen Rou shook her head: 'I... I can't hit...'}

"Liu Yue."

The dictionary at Liu Yue's waist rang: 'Chen Rou, pull He Miao. It doesn't count if it's too light.'

Chen Rou's hand trembled. The hanger fell down, like dusting off. He Miao snorted, but her back didn’t collapse. Xiao Wei walked over, grabbed Chen Rou's wrist, and helped her strike a second time. This time it landed on flesh, making a sound. He Miao's elbow slid away and then pushed back, and finally the book fell to the ground.

"Now that's more like it." Xiaowei let go, her voice still low. "Remember the strength. Next time, if I don't feel like doing it myself, it'll be you. If your hand is too soft, start by hitting yourself until it's hard enough."

Chen Rou cried out. Xiaowei ignored her. She stacked the fallen books back onto He Miao's back, sat back in her chair, and put her feet on He Miao's calves as a second low stool. She opened her notebook and continued writing. Liu Yue reported the time from the doorway. Chen Rou held onto the clothes hanger, standing in place, as if nailed there.

After finishing a page, Xiaowei put the cap on her pen.

"Positions are fixed. He Miao is furniture and dog, Liu Yue is the mouth, Chen Rou is the hands. How the classroom is arranged, I don't care. Once you enter this door, follow this. Whoever takes their position out of the dormitory—"

She glanced at Liu Yue.

Liu Yue responded quickly: 'Who took the position out of the dorm——'

"Then let the other two deal with her according to their positions."

"Then let the other two deal with her according to their positions."

He Miao's back was trembling. The dictionary made a creaking sound behind Liu Yue's waist. The clothes hanger in Chen Rou's hand was as heavy as iron.

Xiaowei listened to these three sounds, and it was actually quieter and clearer than when she imitated those old words. This was written by her. Not copied.

She moved her foot to a different position on He Miao's leg, as if changing the way she was sitting.

"Continue. Report the time."

"Two twenty-two."

"Pass it: Hold the table for another fifteen minutes."

Liu Yue said to He Miao: 'The table, hold on for another fifteen minutes.'

He Miao couldn't respond. Furniture has no mouth. She just lifted her back another inch.

8. See through

The third week of school.

The morning in the dorm still followed its routine. He Miao knelt like a short stool, Liu Yue stared at her phone to check the time, Chen Rou held the hanger until her hands sweated. Xiao Wei rested her foot on that part of her back, her voice not loud:

"Whoever loosens first will undergo an extra check tonight. Liu Yue, repeat it."

"Whoever gives up first will get an extra test tonight."

She changed her clothes and went out. Her back was very straight in the hallway. Once the door closed, the shell was tightly sealed.

The group agreed to meet at the long table by the window in the library. The boy opposite was called Lin Yu, a first-year high school student, leading this group. He spoke little and had a quiet gaze.

Xiaowei opened her notebook, speaking in a familiar tone that left no room for negotiation:

"Follow the plan I mentioned yesterday. You handle the data, and you write the review. Send it to me before midnight tonight."

Some people nodded. Someone glanced at Lin Yu. He didn't respond, he just twirled his pen halfway around. The discussion proceeded at her pace. Her sentences just fell onto the table like that.

When the event ended, others left first. He put the cap back on his pen, speaking just loudly enough for her to hear:

"8 PM, third-floor study room. Bring your computer."

Not asking. Xiaowei wanted to say that she had plans tonight. What came out, however, was:

"...Okay."

It was two minutes to eight. She stood at the stair landing on the third floor, her hand pausing briefly on the doorknob. She pushed it open. The light was white. Lin Yu was already sitting by the window. The chair across from him was pulled out, as if it had been reserved for her all along. A handwritten list was pushed in front of her. Three items, the order clear. The areas she had marked for others in the afternoon had all been narrowed down here.

She hadn't even sat down yet, but she already wanted to take back the set from noon:

"I will arrange this part myself. You just give me the overall one."

Lin Yu didn't argue. He didn't smile either. He just pushed the list forward by half an inch and tapped his fingertip on the first item.

"Sit. Phone on do not disturb. Follow the order. Show me when the second item is done."

Xiaowei looked at that finger. The oppressive words in the dorm reached the tip of her tongue, but when faced with him, she couldn’t use them. She sat down and turned on Do Not Disturb. When the screen went dark, her finger froze. The document still opened according to the first entry. She highlighted the summary according to the section he had marked.

Halfway through, she typed on the keyboard very quickly, as if trying to prove that she could divide, catch up, and overpower this sheet of paper. Lin Yu didn’t lift his eyes, and only said:

"Slow down. Finish reading before writing."

She slowed down. She could even hear the keyboard getting lighter.

After finishing the second one, she turned the screen around. He glanced at it twice:

"Replace this one. Use the one I marked. Don't add a third paragraph yourself."

She changed. She didn't ask why. After finishing the third task, she instinctively wanted to start planning for tomorrow—who would write the introduction, who would collect the data. Lin Yu closed the folder, the clip pressing down on the page she had just finished writing.

"Send it to me. Go back to sleep. You don’t need to handle the group matters tomorrow."

“Okay.”

This word felt lighter than the kind she used at the long table at noon. He didn't ask her to say it again, pulled the chair back, and let her go first.

When returning to the dormitory, He Miao was already lying under the bed. Liu Yue reported the time. Chen Rou held the bowl of porridge in her hands, not yet steady in her kneeling. Xiao Wei put her feet back, her voice still cold:

"Position. Whoever asks me where I went first, will get an extra inspection tonight."

After the lights went out, she stared at the bed slats above. She couldn't understand why she hadn't pushed back. She could handle three people in their positions. With a list, she would complete it in order.

He was very quiet when he looked at her. It wasn't like he was listening to her instructions, more like he was watching to see if she would follow through with the next step.

She did as she was told.

From that day on, she remained the one giving orders in the dormitory. Sitting alone with him, she would lean back into the chair, waiting for him to speak.

She can, just be a bit more obedient.

9. Girlfriend

The group assignment has been submitted. In mid-October, the third floor still has those two seats.

During this month, Lin Yu took on the small, burdensome matters in her life one by one. On the day she bought practice exercises, she stood in front of the bookstore shelves for a long time, with two books having almost identical contents. Her phone buzzed; it was a link from him with only one line: 'This one.' She put the other book back and ordered it on the spot. The review schedule was also marked by him, indicating from which chapter to start and on which days to practice dictation, and she adjusted her calendar accordingly. Only after making the changes did she realize she hadn't asked why.

In the dormitory, she was still giving orders according to her role. One night, Liu Yue repeated a part half a beat late; her voice went cold, and she shoved the dictionary back into that soft waist. The phone lit up on the table. Tonight at eight, third floor. Do not disturb. I’ll wait for you.

She left. She didn't explain why she got angry. Lin Yu pushed a cup of warm water over, with the usual three items on the list. After finishing, he said to go back and sleep. She said okay.

Outsiders only thought that the senior students were leading the group. She herself couldn't say exactly when that boundary disappeared.

On Friday night, the study room was left with only them. The lights were white. Xiaowei closed the last page, but did not get up. She watched his hand put away the pen, her lips moved slightly, and then she spoke. For the first time, she asked for it herself:

"I... kind of want you to keep arranging me like this."

Lin Yu's hand paused. He didn't respond immediately. He zipped up his pencil case, walked around the table, and sat on the chair next to her. His palm faced up, resting in front of her.

"Give me the handle."

She hardly hesitated. Her fingers went up. His fingertip pressed on the back of her hand, as if confirming that this hand would stay here.

"From today on, be my girlfriend."

It wasn't a question. Xiaowei's heart skipped a beat. She looked up. There was no probing there, nor was there any request for her to explain what kind of person she was. It was just the same arrangement: sit, no disturbance, follow the order.

She nodded.

“Okay.”

His other hand came over, still pressing only on the back of the hand. Nothing else was added.

"Leave the tiring work to me. You don't have to share it with anyone else when you're with me."

“Okay.”

They walked together to the bottom of her dormitory building. The wind blew the phoenix tree leaves across the steps. Before parting, he called out to her.

"Send a message every night before bed. What you did today, where you feel uncomfortable, what you want me to arrange for tomorrow. You can also ask about clothes."

Her throat tightened, yet she still answered:

I will.

"Go."

Go upstairs. He Miao was already lying down. Liu Yue called out the time. Chen Rou was kneeling. Xiao Wei put her feet back, her voice still cold:

"Position. Whoever asks me where I went first, will get an extra inspection tonight."

Before the light went out, she curled up in the top bunk and sent it as he said. She did her homework today. No discomfort. Tomorrow's clothes—she stopped typing here, deleted that sentence, and typed again: What to wear tomorrow, you decide.

Reply quickly. White shirt, black long pants. Don't think about anything else. Reply when you arrive.

She typed two characters. Only after sending them did she let Liu Yue turn off the light.

The next morning, she changed into the white shirt and black trousers he had specified. Her eyebrows and eyes still looked cold in the mirror. He Miao was already kneeling like a short stool. She put her foot on him and spoke in a low voice:

"Post. Whoever asks me first about how to change clothes will get extra inspection tonight."

Liu Yue repeats. Chen Rou goes to get a basin. No one knows the message by the pillow. The phone vibrates again when going downstairs. Noon, twelve o'clock, the first canteen by the window. Do not disturb. I am waiting for you.

She put the phone into her pocket. The corridor was still straight. The shell was still in this room. On his side, she was just waiting for the next arrangement.
