## 4. Six-Chapter Plot Design (*Bryce & Dannie*)

### Ch. 1 — The Inspection *(bridges Orig. Ch. 4 flashback → Ch. 4 opening texts)*
- **Focus:** Bryce & Dannie's first real night together, set up by Rob.
- **Beats:**
  - Dannie arrives intending to audit whether Bryce is actually good to her little brother and tries to steer the conversation and pace.

- **Most degrading play:** Psychological / sibling — Dannie deliberately posing with Bryce's cock for a photo sent straight to her little brother while watching Bryce command Lin's mouth by text.

### Ch. 2 — Saturday *(On-screen cameo #1: full harem debut)*
- **Focus:** Fills the unwritten canon Saturday night promised in Orig. Ch. 4.
- **Beats:**
  - Sushi dinner, then back to Bryce's house. For the first time, Dannie watches sharp, independent Lin kneel in a collar and ask Master Bryce for permission in person.
  - Bedroom centerpiece: Bryce directs and fucks **both Asian women together** while Rob watches naked and caged from a chair — establishing the visual template of Bryce's harem.

- **Rob & Lin role:** **On-screen cameo #1 (full room).** Lin models collared obedience to Bryce; Rob sits caged in the chair watching Bryce take both his fiancée and his *jiejie*, then obeys at the Catan table.
- **Most degrading play:** Rob locked naked in a chair watching Bryce fuck his fiancée and big sister side by side as a matched set, followed by Bryce enforcing the *"key in the ocean"* threat over Catan.

### Ch. 3 — Taking the Wheel *(between Orig. Ch. 4 and Ch. 5)*

### Ch. 4 — The Ambush & The Beating *(wedding / honeymoon departure window)*
- **Focus:** Bryce, Dannie, and Matt.
- **Beats:**
  - While Rob & Lin are occupied with wedding/honeymoon logistics (kept completely off-screen so Dannie protects Rob's wedding week), **Matt** resurfaces in town.
  - Two years of lifting, money, and bro-culture convinced Matt he is finally an "alpha." Furious that Dannie is with a white guy and determined to erase his college shame, he tracks Dannie to Bryce's warehouse gym/house while Bryce is in the back/shower and demands she delete the old photos.

When Matt threatens to call the police, Dannie calmly walks him through the footage: it is textbook defense of another and self-defense — calling the cops puts *Matt* in handcuffs, right before his family sees both the arrest and the college photos.

- **Rob & Lin role:** Off-screen (Dannie deliberately shields Rob so his wedding/honeymoon stays clean).
- **Most degrading play (non-sexual):** Total physical demolition of Matt's fake-alpha delusion in front of Dannie — beaten bloody onto the mats, begging Bryce to stop, then realizing the security footage makes him the sole criminal.

- **Beats:**
